# Sprint 1 Review and Retrospective

## Sprint Review

### Completed Features
- User Management.

### Sprint Goal Review
At the end of the sprint, the team met with the mentors to verify whether the sprint objectives were achieved. After reviewing the progress, the team evaluated the current situation and planned the next steps based on the feedback received.

During the Sprint 1 review, the mentors highlighted that the frontend code structure was unclear and lacked a proper organization system. Additionally, the Path Builder feature was not fully completed within this sprint.

## Sprint Retrospective

### What Went Well
- The Git workflow was clear and successfully followed by the team.
- Task distribution was well-defined.
- Backend development progressed efficiently.

### Challenges Faced
- Communication challenges occurred because most team members had university and work commitments.
- Jira slowed down the workflow and made task management and communication more complicated.
- Frontend and backend teams were initially working separately, which reduced coordination between both sides.

### Improvements for the Next Sprint
- Replace Jira with a workflow that better fits the team's communication style.
- Establish suitable meeting times and use Discord communication channels when team members cannot attend meetings.
- Restructure the team workflow by pairing backend and frontend developers to work together on the same feature.
