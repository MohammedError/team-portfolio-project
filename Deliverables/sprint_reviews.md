# Sprint 2 Progress Monitoring

## Daily Stand-ups
Daily stand-up meetings continued through Discord. The team used these meetings to review progress, discuss challenges, and coordinate upcoming tasks.

## Task Tracking
Jira was replaced with a Discord-based workflow after the team noticed that Jira introduced additional overhead and slowed down communication. The new approach used dedicated Discord channels for different development areas:

- Frontend channel.
- UI/UX channel.
- Backend channel.
- Commits channel.

This approach improved transparency, allowed all team members to follow development updates, and made collaboration faster when assistance was needed.

## Metrics

- Completed tasks: 4
- Identified bugs: 0
- Resolved bugs: 0

## Adjustments
The team adapted the task management process by replacing Jira with a communication-based workflow that better matched the team's development style and improved collaboration efficiency.
